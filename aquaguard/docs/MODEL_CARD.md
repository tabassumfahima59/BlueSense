# Model card — AquaGuard scarcity forecast

_Generated by `ml/model_card.py` from `ml/artifacts/metrics.json`; do not edit by hand._

## What it predicts
The monthly **climatic water balance D = P − ET0 (mm/month)** per division, 1, 3 and 6 months ahead. P and ET0 come from ERA5 reanalysis (Open-Meteo, one point per division). D is a measurable quantity; AquaGuard does not train on a synthetic 'scarcity index'.

## Data and split
- Data period: 1991-01 to 2026-10 (8 divisions, monthly).
- Split by target date, never random: train: target year <= 2016; validation: 2017-2020; test: 2021+.
- Samples per horizon: h=1: train 2400, validation 384, test 560; h=3: train 2384, validation 384, test 560; h=6: train 2360, validation 384, test 560.
- Trained: 2026-11-12T16:42:28+00:00. Evaluated: 2026-11-12T16:42:52+00:00.
- Features (no information from the forecast period): lags 0–11 of P, ET0 and D; Tmean lags 0–2; soil moisture (ERA5-Land model data); rolling means; D anomaly vs training-period climatology; target-month sin/cos; training-period normal and spread of D for the target month; division id.

## Models
Random Forest and XGBoost are fit on the training years; the one with the lower validation RMSE is kept, refit on train + validation and scored on the test years. The operational model is then refit on all labelled data. Baselines: climatology (training-period mean of that month) and persistence (last observed D).

## Reading these numbers honestly
- The selected model improves on climatology only modestly at 1 month and roughly matches it at longer horizons; monthly rainfall in Bangladesh is weakly predictable months ahead.
- Dryness classes: the model rarely predicts the dry classes, so its macro-F1 is close to the climatology baseline. The risk class therefore leans on the engine's stress ratio (rule: level = max(stress, dryness)).
- Interval coverage below the nominal 90% means the per-month residual intervals are too narrow on the test years.

## Uncertainty and explainability
90% intervals from 5th–95th percentiles of validation residuals per target month. SHAP TreeExplainer on the operational model: global mean |SHAP| over test rows and a local explanation of each division's latest forecast (ml/artifacts/shap.json).

## Intended use and limits
Decision support and education: indicating whether the coming months are likely drier or wetter than normal for a division. Not for operational drought declaration, not station-level, and not a forecast of water availability or groundwater. Under a What-if scenario the model is re-run with perturbed recent inputs plus the scenario's direct effect on the target month; that output is Simulated, not a forecast.
