# BlueSense submission video script

Target duration: **2 minutes 45 seconds**, inside the official checklist's 2-3 minute range. Script only: no video has been recorded or uploaded. Rehearse against real working data before recording. If a screen is unavailable, repair it or accurately show its unavailable state; do not edit simulated outputs into apparent live evidence.

| Time | Screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:15 | Title and Command Center | "BlueSense: predict water risk, protect every drop. We help agricultural advisers and water planners in Bangladesh bring public-source data, climate predictions and what-if comparisons into one workspace." |
| 0:15-0:45 | Region selector and Water Rhythm | "Here we choose a division and inspect its rainfall history and climatic water balance. Source dates and spatial scope matter: weather represents a divisional point, while national statistics stay national. Water Rhythm lets us compare months without filling missing observations." |
| 0:45-1:15 | What-if and derived budget | "Now we compare a dry-year scenario with the baseline. These changed results are simulations. The derived water budget reconciles supply, delivered water and losses; allocation optimization shows a proposal under stated assumptions, rather than an observed outcome." |
| 1:15-1:45 | Prediction Lab, then configured Copilot if verified | "Our prediction target is precipitation minus reference evapotranspiration. We compare it with climatology and persistence, show uncertainty and acknowledge modest skill. The Copilot uses validated read-only backend tools to explain available evidence. Unsupported questions should remain unanswered." |
| 1:45-2:10 | Smart Irrigation, or labelled conceptual Water Flow | "For a supported crop and location, irrigation estimates combine forecast weather, crop coefficients, rainfall and efficiency. The recommendation needs local validation. Our separate water-flow view is a hypothetical simulation, not a real sensor network or physical leak detector." |
| 2:10-2:35 | Architecture diagram and repository | "The product connects a Next.js interface, FastAPI services, shared water calculations and SQLite provenance, with offline machine-learning artifacts and configurable cloud AI. Our public repository includes setup instructions, methods, model evaluation and explicit limitations." |
| 2:35-2:45 | Closing title and verified links | "BlueSense makes water evidence easier to inspect and compare. Predict water risk, protect every drop." |

## Recording notes

Use clear audio, readable zoom and actual responses. For the Copilot segment, configure and verify a provider first; otherwise describe and show the real not-configured state. Show only the implemented architecture, not planned RAG/MCP components. Avoid showing .env files, keys, private browser tabs or beneficiary/personal information. End with the actual repository/live URLs, then upload to YouTube and verify judge access and playback. The pitch-deck outline is a separate artifact, not a replacement for the working-software demonstration.
